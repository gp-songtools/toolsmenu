Spec to Suno

Send a song spec to Suno from Claude

Which should I use?

  • The Claude skill is simpler for casual use: one upload, then ask for a song in any chat.
  • The page is a form with sliders and a request preview. It needs to be published as a page in your own Claude.

Option 1: the page (spec-to-suno.html)

Download spec-to-suno.html

A single page you publish in your own Claude account. It only works there: opened straight from your computer or another website, it just tells you to open it in claude.ai.

What it does

It loads a song spec file, lets you adjust Suno settings (tempo, key, title, excluded styles, vocal gender, duration, Max mode, weirdness, style influence, variety), shows you the exact request, and sends it to your Suno account. It can also request loops. Suno's results include clip IDs and links to the songs and cover art.

What you need

  • A Claude account that can publish pages.
  • A sign-up for the Suno MCP, Suno's connector for Claude. Without it, the page can't connect to Suno.
  • The Suno connector added in Claude (Settings, then Connectors).
  • A Suno plan for what you want to make. Loops need a Pro plan or higher.

Everything is billed to your own Suno account and Claude usage.

Setup

  1. Download spec-to-suno.html from this page.
  2. Open a Claude chat and attach the file.
  3. Ask Claude:

    “Publish this attached HTML file as a page, and let it use my Suno connector. It needs these Suno tools: audio-create, audio-covers-create, sounds-create, audio-retrieve and account-usage. It also needs the ability to ask Claude for suggestions, and to offer file downloads.”

  4. Claude may ask you to confirm, because the page asks to use your Suno connector. Confirm that you want it.
  5. Open the page, choose your song spec file, adjust settings, and press Generate. Check the request shown, then press Send to Suno.

Limits

  • Cover art appears as links, not images, because published pages can't load images from other sites.
  • It does not play audio. Open the song link on suno.com to listen.
  • It must be published from your own Claude account. If you share someone else's published copy, it may not be able to reach your Suno connector.
  • It has only been tested with its author's Claude setup. If publishing doesn't work for you, this page can't say why.

The page contains no passwords or keys, and it only sends requests you confirm.

Option 2: the Claude skill (suno-from-spec.zip)

Suno from a song spec: a Claude skill

Download suno-from-spec.zip

A skill you add to your own Claude account. Upload the ZIP as it is: don't unzip it.

What it does

After you add it to Claude, attach a song spec file in a chat and ask for a song, cover or loop. Claude asks about each Suno setting (or uses the spec's values, or suggests settings), shows you the exact request, waits for your OK, then sends it to your Suno account and plays both results in the chat. Suno's results include clip IDs and links to the songs and cover art.

What you need

  • A Claude account that allows custom skills.
  • A sign-up for the Suno MCP, Suno's connector for Claude. Without it, the skill can't connect to Suno.
  • The Suno connector added in Claude (Settings, then Connectors).
  • A Suno plan for what you want to make. Loops need a Pro plan or higher.

Everything is billed to your own Suno account.

Setup

  1. Download suno-from-spec.zip from this page. Do not unzip it.
  2. In Claude, open Customize, then Skills, and add or upload a skill. Choose the ZIP file.
  3. Make sure the skill is switched on in the skills list.
  4. Turn on the Suno connector in Claude's connector settings.
  5. Start a new chat, attach a song spec file, and ask:

    “Make a song from this spec.”

  6. Answer Claude's questions about the settings, check the request it shows, and say yes to send it.

Limits

  • Cover art comes as links. Claude gives you the link; it doesn't show the picture in chat.
  • The song player appears in the chat when your Claude app supports it. It may not appear everywhere.
  • Custom skills belong to one person. If you want a friend to use it, send them this page, and they upload their own copy.
  • Menu names change over time. If a step doesn't match your screen, check Claude's help page Use skills in Claude.
  • It has only been tested by its author. If something doesn't work for you, this page can't say why.

The skill contains no passwords or keys, and it only sends requests you confirm.

Blank spec files

Both options read the same song spec files. Start from a blank template, or follow the format in songspec/SPEC.md.

Blank song Blank cover Blank loop (sound)

A cover needs the Suno clip ID in cover_source, and a loop needs a description in its Sound section. The page offers the same templates inside it. The GP Song Tools can also fill a spec in for you: each has a Song spec panel with a Download button.
