Privacy & credits

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This page covers every tool at gp-songtools.github.io: the tools menu, Lyricpad, Suno Style Creator, Earprint and GP Mastering. None of them has accounts, a server of its own or a database. Your songs, lyrics, audio and settings stay in your own browser unless you choose a feature that sends them somewhere, as described below.

Visit statistics (all tools)

Every page loads Cloudflare Web Analytics so we can see how many people use each tool. It reports, in aggregate:

  • which page was visited, and the site you came from;
  • your browser, operating system and device type;
  • your country, worked out from your IP address;
  • how quickly the page loaded.

Cloudflare states that Web Analytics uses no cookies or other browser storage, does not fingerprint visitors, and does not use your IP address to identify or track you. We see totals only, never individual visitors. It never sees your lyrics, audio, songs, API keys or anything you type: those are not part of a page visit. Cloudflare's privacy policy applies to this data.

Content blockers and privacy extensions usually block it. The tools work exactly the same either way.

Hosting

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads a page; see GitHub's privacy statement.

What each tool sends, and where

Tools menu

Nothing beyond the visit statistics above.

Lyricpad

Lyrics and settings stay in your browser. Saving to Google Drive and AI line suggestions are optional. Lyricpad has its own privacy policy with the full details, including how it uses Google Drive.

Suno Style Creator

  • Gemini: when you press Generate, the artist, song, topic, pasted lyrics and any YouTube Music link go from your browser directly to Google's Gemini API under your own key. Google's privacy policy and the Gemini API terms apply.
  • MusicAtlas (optional, on by default): the artist and song names are sent to MusicAtlas for audio analysis. The request passes through a small relay we run on Cloudflare Workers, because MusicAtlas cannot be called from a browser directly. The relay forwards the request and stores nothing. You can turn MusicAtlas off in the form.
  • Settings: your model choice and MusicAtlas settings are kept in your browser.

Earprint

  • Measurements (tempo, key, loudness, mood) are made in your browser. Audio is not uploaded for these.
  • AI review (optional): when you ask for one, Earprint sends Gemini the measurements, the file name and any lyrics you pasted, plus, if listening is on, either the first six minutes of your file (16 kHz mono) or the YouTube link you gave. It goes from your browser directly to Google under your own key. For a Spotify link, only the artist and title are sent. The full list is in Earprint's README.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address.

GP Mastering

  • Audio is processed in your browser and never uploaded, including stem separation.
  • AI suggestions (optional): only when you press Analyze, a compressed copy of the song goes from your browser directly to Google's Gemini API under your own key.
  • Stored on your device: settings, saved setups and reference measurements (numbers only, never audio) in browser storage, and separated stems in your browser's database so they don't need separating again.
  • Downloads: the MP3 and M4A encoders load from the cdnjs and jsDelivr code hosts, and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any host, they see your IP address.

Your API keys

The AI features use your own Gemini or Claude API key. Keys are sent only to the provider they belong to, straight from your browser. They are kept in memory and forgotten when you close the tab, unless you tick the tool's "Remember" option, which saves the key in this browser's storage on this device only. Because all the tools share the gp-songtools.github.io address, a remembered key can be read by any page on it, so don't tick Remember on a shared computer.
