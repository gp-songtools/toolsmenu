Privacy & credits

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This page covers every tool at gp-songtools.github.io: the tools menu, Lyricpad, GP Blueprint (formerly Suno Style Creator), Earprint, GP Mastering, AuraLyrics Studio, Randomash, Idea Wizard, GP Producer, Framecut and Spec to Suno. None of the tools has accounts, a server of its own or a database; the one exception is the optional hosted Spec to Suno, described below. Your songs, lyrics, audio and settings stay in your own browser unless you choose a feature that sends them somewhere, as described below.

Visit statistics (all tools)

Every page on this site and in every tool loads Cloudflare Web Analytics so we can see how many people use each tool. It reports, in aggregate:

  • which page was visited, and the site you came from;
  • your browser, operating system and device type;
  • your country, worked out from your IP address;
  • how quickly the page loaded.

Cloudflare states that Web Analytics uses no cookies or other browser storage, does not fingerprint visitors, and does not use your IP address to identify or track you. We see totals only, never individual visitors. It never sees your lyrics, audio, songs, API keys or anything you type: those are not part of a page visit. Cloudflare’s privacy policy applies to this data.

Content blockers and privacy extensions usually block it. The tools work exactly the same either way.

Hosting

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads a page; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

What each tool sends, and where

Tools menu

Nothing beyond the visit statistics above.

The song spec (shared by the tools)

Tools that support the song spec keep one shared copy of the song you’re working on (style, structure, lyrics and so on, whatever the tools have saved) in your browser’s storage, so the next tool you open can read it. It stays in this browser on this device and is never sent anywhere by itself. A tool sends parts of it to an AI provider only when you choose to use them there, as described for each tool below. Download saves it as a file on your device; New song empties it. The format is documented in the song spec folder.

Google Drive (optional). Where a tool’s Song spec panel shows Save to Drive and Open from Drive…, you can keep the song spec in your own Google Drive, in a GP Song Tools folder with one folder per song, and open it on another device. Nothing is loaded or sent to Google until you click one of those buttons; then Google’s sign-in script loads from accounts.google.com and you sign in with Google. The tools ask only for the drive.file permission, so they can see just the files and folders GP Song Tools created, not the rest of your Drive. What goes to Google Drive is the song spec text and the folder and file names (the song title). Your browser stores the sign-in token (valid for up to an hour, shared by the GP Song Tools on this site) and a note of which Drive copy was last synced; Sign out of Drive removes both. None of this goes to us or to any other server.

Lyricpad

Lyrics and settings stay in your browser. Saving to Google Drive and AI line suggestions are optional. Lyricpad has its own privacy policy with the full details, including how it uses Google Drive.

GP Blueprint

  • Gemini: when you press Generate, the artist, song, topic, pasted lyrics and any YouTube Music link go from your browser directly to Google’s Gemini API under your own key. Google’s privacy policy and the Gemini API terms apply.
  • MusicAtlas (optional, on by default): the artist and song names are sent to MusicAtlas for audio analysis. The request passes through a small relay we run on Cloudflare Workers, because MusicAtlas cannot be called from a browser directly. The relay forwards the request and stores nothing. You can turn MusicAtlas off in the form.
  • Song spec: if you tick “Add Earprint’s analysis to the prompt” or press Use on the song spec’s topic or lyrics, that text goes to Gemini with the rest of the request. “Save to song spec” stores the result, the artist, song and YouTube link you used (marked “never send to Suno”) and, if ticked, the BPM and key in the shared song spec in your browser.
  • Settings: your model choice and MusicAtlas settings are kept in your browser.

Earprint

  • Measurements (tempo, key, loudness, mood) are made in your browser. Audio is not uploaded for these.
  • AI review (optional): when you ask for one, Earprint sends Gemini the measurements, the file name and any lyrics you pasted, plus, if listening is on, either the first six minutes of your file (16 kHz mono) or the YouTube link you gave. It goes from your browser directly to Google under your own key. For a Spotify link, only the artist and title are sent. The full list is in Earprint’s README.
  • Song spec: Use these lyrics copies Lyricpad’s lyrics from the shared song spec into the lyrics box, which goes to Gemini with a review like anything you paste. Save to song spec stores the analysis and, only if you tick it, the BPM and key in the shared song spec in your browser.
  • Google Drive (optional): Earprint’s Song spec panel offers Save to Drive and Open from Drive…, as described under the song spec above; nothing goes to Google until you click one.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address.

GP Mastering

  • Audio is processed in your browser and never uploaded, except by the two optional features below.
  • Stem separation on your own server (optional): stem separation runs in your browser unless you choose Separate on: My server. Then, when you press Separate, the section you chose goes as 16-bit audio to the server address you entered, and the stems come back. That is a server you run yourself (for example on Modal, whose own privacy terms then apply); GP Song Tools has no server of its own and never sees it. Its access token is sent only to that server, and is kept like an API key (see Your API keys).
  • AI suggestions (optional): only when you press Analyze, a compressed copy of the song goes from your browser directly to Google’s Gemini API under your own key.
  • Stored on your device: settings, saved setups and reference measurements (numbers only, never audio) in browser storage, and separated stems in your browser’s database so they don’t need separating again.
  • Downloads: the MP3 and M4A encoders load from the cdnjs and jsDelivr code hosts, and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any host, they see your IP address.

AuraLyrics Studio

  • Audio and video: your song is played, visualised and exported as a video entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded for this. When a fast render starts, the video library (Mediabunny) downloads from the jsDelivr code host, which sees your IP address.
  • In-browser transcription (optional): the Whisper speech-recognition model runs on your device. The first time, the transcription library downloads from the jsDelivr code host and the model from Hugging Face; your audio stays on your device.
  • Cloud transcription (optional): if you choose Groq or OpenAI, the audio (converted to 16 kHz mono) goes from your browser directly to that provider under your own API key. Groq’s or OpenAI’s privacy policy applies.
  • Stored on your device: your settings, custom colour palettes, any background or cover image you add and your chosen Whisper model, in this browser’s storage. Your Groq or OpenAI key is saved there only if you tick “Remember key on this device”.
  • Song spec: AuraLyrics only reads it. Use these lyrics copies Lyricpad’s lyrics from the shared song spec in your browser into the lyric editor; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Send to Framecut: when you click the button, timed lyrics and the soundtrack filename are shared with Framecut through this browser’s storage. Framecut clears the handoff when you import or dismiss it and discards handoffs older than a day when it checks for them. Download LRC saves a local lyrics file.
  • Downloads: page styling loads from the Tailwind CSS code host and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any host, they see your IP address.

Randomash

  • Dry run is on by default: it shows the request and its cost and sends nothing.
  • Generating: with Dry run off, when you press Generate, the prompt and your settings (tempo, key, seed and so on) go from your browser directly to Google’s Gemini API under your own key, and the music comes back. Google’s privacy policy and the Gemini API terms apply. Google marks generated music with its SynthID watermark.
  • Song spec download: made in your browser from your mix and prompt, and saved to your device. Nothing is sent.
  • Stem splitting (optional): runs on your device with GP Mastering’s splitter, which downloads its models from this site the first time.
  • Stored on your device: your history, clips and trims in this browser’s database, your settings and your balance estimate in browser storage, and your key only if you tick “Remember key”. If you choose an output folder (Chrome or Edge), runs are also saved there, on your own disk.
  • Downloads: fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address.

Idea Wizard

  • Asking for ideas: only when you press Conjure 3 ideas, More ideas, Expand or Go deeper, what you pasted (style prompt, song structure, Earprint analysis, your direction) and the idea you are working on go from your browser directly to the AI provider you chose, under your own key: Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini; privacy policy and API terms) or Groq. Their privacy terms apply. Your key is also used to list the models it can reach.
  • Stored on your device: your inputs, chosen provider and model, in this browser’s storage. Your key is saved there only if you tick “Remember key on this device”.
  • Song spec: Use copies GP Blueprint’s style prompt and structure or Earprint’s analysis from the shared song spec into the boxes, which then go to the AI provider like anything you paste. Save to song spec stores the idea and its song structure in the shared song spec in your browser.

GP Producer

  • Claude: only when you press a button that asks it to work (for example Generate blueprint, Ask Claude to fix or Ask Claude to revise), your lyrics, your vision text, any audio brief and the scene prompts go from your browser directly to Anthropic under your own key. Anthropic’s privacy terms apply.
  • Gemini images: when you generate an anchor or storyboard frame, its image prompt, and any reference images that go with it, go to Google’s Gemini API under your own key. Google’s privacy policy and the Gemini API terms apply. Test key & list models in Settings sends your Gemini key to Google to see which models it can use.
  • Audio (optional): your song file is sent to Google only when you press Generate with Gemini in the audio brief, and only after you confirm the first time. Added under Local audio, it otherwise stays in the browser tab’s memory: it is not saved, not exported and not sent anywhere.
  • Google Drive (optional): after you click Save clips to Drive on the Production tab (or a Drive button in the Song spec panel, as described under the song spec above), GP Producer saves your video clips, the latest anchor and keyframe images and the project .zip into the song’s GP Song Tools folder in your own Google Drive. It asks only for the drive.file permission. Nothing goes to Google Drive until you click, and your Anthropic and Gemini keys are never sent there. Clip videos are kept only in the browser tab’s memory until you save them; they are not stored in the browser.
  • No other requests: the app’s Content-Security-Policy blocks connections to any address except Anthropic, Google (Gemini, and Google sign-in and the Drive API for the optional Drive buttons) and the visit statistics. It loads no fonts or scripts from other hosts, apart from Google’s sign-in script after you click a Drive button. Apart from the visit statistics above, it makes no other requests. GP Producer makes no videos: you take its prompts to Google Flow yourself, which is outside these tools.
  • Stored on your device: your Anthropic and Gemini keys are saved in this browser’s storage in plain text, as soon as you enter them (there is no Remember option; see Your API keys), along with your settings and whether you agreed to send audio to Gemini. Projects, scenes, reference images and frames are stored in this browser’s database (IndexedDB), on this computer only.
  • Deleting it: Forget keys in Settings removes both keys, and Delete on the project list removes a project. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes everything. Export whole project (.zip) and Download export bundle (.zip) save files on your device and never contain your keys or your audio.
  • Song spec: Use as lyrics, Use as runtime and Use as project name copy from the shared song spec in your browser into the project; the lyrics then go to Anthropic like any lyrics you paste. Save video sequence to song spec writes the scene list there, in your browser, and sends nothing.

Framecut

  • Editing and export: your video, images and audio are read, edited and exported entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Background removal, body effects and the video library run on your device, and everything they need (models, fonts, Mediabunny, Whisper) is built into the page, so Framecut downloads no code or models from other hosts.
  • In-browser transcription: In-browser Whisper runs the bundled Whisper Tiny model on your device. Your audio stays on your device.
  • Cloud transcription (optional): if you choose Whisper AI Cloud and press Transcribe, the selected clip’s trimmed audio goes from your browser directly to Groq or OpenAI, whichever you pick, under your own API key. That provider’s privacy policy applies.
  • Live Browser Listener (optional): the clip is played through your browser’s own speech recognition service. Framecut sends it nowhere itself; what that service does with audio is up to your browser, so check its privacy terms.
  • Stored on your device: your Groq or OpenAI key is saved in this browser’s storage only if you tick “Remember API key on this device” (it starts off), and Forget saved key removes it. Other pages on gp-songtools.github.io can read saved keys. Framecut keeps no other settings or media in browser storage. Save Project downloads a .framecut file with your media and edits to your device.
  • Song spec: Use Song Spec Lyrics and Use Song Spec Timed Lyrics copy from the shared song spec in your browser into the importer, and Save Timed Lyrics to Song Spec writes the timed lines back to it. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Google Drive (optional): nothing goes to Google until you click a Drive button. Place clips from Drive… reads the clip files in the song’s GP Song Tools folder on your Google Drive and downloads only the ones you place. Save to Google Drive too in the export window, and the Drive menu (Save project to Drive, Open project from Drive…, Save last export to Drive), save exports and .framecut project files (with your media) into that song’s exports and projects folders. Framecut asks Google only for access to files and folders the GP Song Tools created (drive.file), and the Song spec panel’s Save to Drive and Open from Drive… work as described under the song spec above. The Save to Google Drive too tick box is remembered in this browser’s storage.
  • From AuraLyrics Studio: when you click Send to Framecut there, the timed lyrics and soundtrack filename are passed through this browser’s storage. Framecut clears the hand-off when you import or dismiss it, and discards ones older than a day.
  • No other requests: Apart from the visit statistics above and Google sign-in and the Google Drive API for the optional Drive buttons (the sign-in script loads only after you click one), Framecut loads no fonts or scripts from other hosts.

Spec to Suno

  • The files on this site: this site only offers two downloads (a Claude skill and a page). They don’t run here, and nothing is sent from this site when you download them.
  • The skill (in your own Claude): you add it to your own Claude account. When you ask for a song in a chat, Claude reads the song spec you attached and, only after you confirm the request, sends it to your own Suno account through the Suno connector. The skill contains no passwords or keys.
  • The page (in your own Claude): you publish it as a page in your own Claude account, where it runs. It reads the song spec file you choose and, only after you confirm a request, sends that request to your own Suno account through the Suno connector you added in Claude. Anthropic’s and Suno’s privacy terms apply to that. The page contains no passwords or keys.

Hosted Spec to Suno (experimental)

A separate web page at suno-oauth-poc.pcarenza.workers.dev, run by the owner on Cloudflare Workers. Unlike the tools above, it has a small server. It has its own privacy page, which is the one to trust if the two ever differ.

  • Signing in: you press Connect Suno and sign in on Suno’s own page (OAuth with PKCE). The site never sees your Suno password.
  • What it stores: your Suno access and refresh tokens, encrypted (AES-GCM) in Cloudflare KV, tied to a random session id kept in an HttpOnly cookie in your browser. A session lasts up to 7 days after your sign-in or the last token refresh, then it is deleted.
  • What passes through: the song spec you open (file picker or drag and drop; it does not read the shared copy in your browser) is read in your browser. Only the request you approve, with its lyrics and prompts, passes through the Worker to Suno. It is not stored.
  • What it can do: use your Suno credits for requests you send. It can call only five Suno functions (create a song, create a cover, create a sound or loop, retrieve a clip, read your plan and credits) and does not read your library. Limits: 6 creation requests a minute and 60 a day per session.
  • Removing it: Disconnect deletes your session and asks Suno to revoke the token when Suno supports it; otherwise remove the app in your Suno account settings.
  • Other requests: Cloudflare keeps standard request logs. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts. It has no analytics.

Your API keys

The AI features use your own Gemini, Claude, Groq or OpenAI API key. Keys are sent only to the provider they belong to, straight from your browser. GP Mastering’s separation server token is handled the same way and sent only to your own server. Apart from GP Producer, the tools keep a key in memory and forget it when you close the tab unless the tool’s “Remember” option is on, which saves the key in this browser’s storage on this device only. That option starts on in Lyricpad and off in the other tools, including Framecut. GP Producer has no Remember option: it saves your Anthropic and Gemini keys in this browser’s storage, in plain text, as soon as you enter them, until you press Forget keys in its Settings. It sends them only to Anthropic and Google. Because all the tools share the gp-songtools.github.io address, a remembered key can be read by any page on it, so turn Remember off on a shared computer.
