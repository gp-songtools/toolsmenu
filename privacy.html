Privacy & credits

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This page covers every tool at gp-songtools.github.io: the tools menu, Lyricpad, GP Blueprint (formerly Suno Style Creator), Earprint, GP Mastering, AuraLyrics Studio and Randomash. None of them has accounts, a server of its own or a database. Your songs, lyrics, audio and settings stay in your own browser unless you choose a feature that sends them somewhere, as described below.

Visit statistics (all tools)

Every page loads Cloudflare Web Analytics so we can see how many people use each tool. It reports, in aggregate:

  • which page was visited, and the site you came from;
  • your browser, operating system and device type;
  • your country, worked out from your IP address;
  • how quickly the page loaded.

Cloudflare states that Web Analytics uses no cookies or other browser storage, does not fingerprint visitors, and does not use your IP address to identify or track you. We see totals only, never individual visitors. It never sees your lyrics, audio, songs, API keys or anything you type: those are not part of a page visit. Cloudflare's privacy policy applies to this data.

Content blockers and privacy extensions usually block it. The tools work exactly the same either way.

Hosting

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads a page; see GitHub's privacy statement.

What each tool sends, and where

Tools menu

Nothing beyond the visit statistics above.

Lyricpad

Lyrics and settings stay in your browser. Saving to Google Drive and AI line suggestions are optional. Lyricpad has its own privacy policy with the full details, including how it uses Google Drive.

GP Blueprint

  • Gemini: when you press Generate, the artist, song, topic, pasted lyrics and any YouTube Music link go from your browser directly to Google's Gemini API under your own key. Google's privacy policy and the Gemini API terms apply.
  • MusicAtlas (optional, on by default): the artist and song names are sent to MusicAtlas for audio analysis. The request passes through a small relay we run on Cloudflare Workers, because MusicAtlas cannot be called from a browser directly. The relay forwards the request and stores nothing. You can turn MusicAtlas off in the form.
  • Settings: your model choice and MusicAtlas settings are kept in your browser.

Earprint

  • Measurements (tempo, key, loudness, mood) are made in your browser. Audio is not uploaded for these.
  • AI review (optional): when you ask for one, Earprint sends Gemini the measurements, the file name and any lyrics you pasted, plus, if listening is on, either the first six minutes of your file (16 kHz mono) or the YouTube link you gave. It goes from your browser directly to Google under your own key. For a Spotify link, only the artist and title are sent. The full list is in Earprint's README.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address.

GP Mastering

  • Audio is processed in your browser and never uploaded, except by the two optional features below.
  • Stem separation on your own server (optional): stem separation runs in your browser unless you choose Separate on: My server. Then, when you press Separate, the section you chose goes as 16-bit audio to the server address you entered, and the stems come back. That is a server you run yourself (for example on Modal, whose own privacy terms then apply); GP Song Tools has no server of its own and never sees it. Its access token is sent only to that server, and is kept like an API key (see Your API keys).
  • AI suggestions (optional): only when you press Analyze, a compressed copy of the song goes from your browser directly to Google's Gemini API under your own key.
  • Stored on your device: settings, saved setups and reference measurements (numbers only, never audio) in browser storage, and separated stems in your browser's database so they don't need separating again.
  • Downloads: the MP3 and M4A encoders load from the cdnjs and jsDelivr code hosts, and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any host, they see your IP address.

AuraLyrics Studio

  • Audio and video: your song is played, visualised and exported as a video entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded for this.
  • In-browser transcription (optional): the Whisper speech-recognition model runs on your device. The first time, the transcription library downloads from the jsDelivr code host and the model from Hugging Face; your audio stays on your device.
  • Cloud transcription (optional): if you choose Groq or OpenAI, the audio (converted to 16 kHz mono) goes from your browser directly to that provider under your own API key. Groq's or OpenAI's privacy policy applies.
  • Stored on your device: your settings, custom colour palettes, any background or cover image you add and your chosen Whisper model, in this browser's storage. Your Groq or OpenAI key is saved there only if you tick "Remember key on this device".
  • Downloads: page styling loads from the Tailwind CSS code host and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any host, they see your IP address.

Randomash

  • Dry run is on by default: it shows the request and its cost and sends nothing.
  • Generating: with Dry run off, when you press Generate, the prompt and your settings (tempo, key, seed and so on) go from your browser directly to Google's Gemini API under your own key, and the music comes back. Google's privacy policy and the Gemini API terms apply. Google marks generated music with its SynthID watermark.
  • Stem splitting (optional): runs on your device with GP Mastering's splitter, which downloads its models from this site the first time.
  • Stored on your device: your history, clips and trims in this browser's database, your settings and your balance estimate in browser storage, and your key only if you tick "Remember key". If you choose an output folder (Chrome or Edge), runs are also saved there, on your own disk.
  • Downloads: fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address.

Your API keys

The AI features use your own Gemini, Claude, Groq or OpenAI API key. Keys are sent only to the provider they belong to, straight from your browser. GP Mastering’s separation server token is handled the same way and sent only to your own server. They are kept in memory and forgotten when you close the tab unless the tool's "Remember" option is on, which saves the key in this browser's storage on this device only. That option starts on in Lyricpad and off in the other tools. Because all the tools share the gp-songtools.github.io address, a remembered key can be read by any page on it, so turn Remember off on a shared computer.
